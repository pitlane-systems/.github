## PitLane Systems LLC builds AI-powered broadcast production software for sim racing. The company was founded in 2026 by Gray Marshall and is based in Austin, Texas.

Its first product, PitLane Director AC, is a standalone Windows application that lets one person produce a race broadcast that would otherwise require a full television crew. Itcombines AI-assisted camera direction, a library of advanced broadcast overlays, and OBS scene automation in a single window, and it runs entirely on the broadcaster's PC with nothing to install on the racers' machines. The product is built for Assetto Corsa league racing, with support for additional titles planned.
PitLane Systems is the official broadcasting partner of Longhorn Sim Racing at the University of Texas at Austin. Founder Gray Marshall was selected as a 2026 McCombs Entrepreneur Summer Fellow by the Harkey Institute for Entrepreneurial Studies at the McCombs School of Business. PitLane Director AC is in closed beta ahead of public launch.
